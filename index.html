How it works

Most cross-venue "arbitrage" is a mirage: different settlement rules, different granularity, or books too thin to fill. PairWatch is built to reject all three.

STEP 1

Verify it's the same question

Every pair is matched and then verified by hand. Near-identical markets that differ in payoff structure, measurement, or timing are rejected — and the rejection is documented, not hidden.

STEP 2

Compare the rules, line by line

Rounding clauses, cancellation rules, resolution sources, close times. If the two venues can settle the "same" event differently, the alert says so — before you trade, not after.

STEP 3

Alert only on fillable edge

Books on both venues are walked at 500 shares. An alert fires only if the net spread is ≥ 0.5% after both venues' fees and both legs fully fill. Checks run about every 15 minutes; maximum 5 alerts a day.

Trap cases

Looks like the same question. It isn't. From the rejected-pairs library (11 cases and counting):

Bitcoin ranges

Polymarket lists daily ranges $2,000 wide. Kalshi lists hourly buckets $100 wide. Same underlying, completely different bets — no pair, no alert.

Fed +25bp, with a twist

Polymarket rounds a non-standard rate change up to the nearest 25bp bracket. Kalshi has no equivalent clause. A 20bp hike can settle as different outcomes on the two venues — every Fed alert carries this warning.

Core CPI vs headline CPI

One venue's "CPI" market tracks core inflation, the other's tracks headline. Similar titles, different numbers, different settlements. Rejected.

Standards

  • Read-only. PairWatch never trades, never holds funds, never asks for your keys or wallet.
  • Fees and depth included. Spreads are computed net of both venues' taker fees, walking real order books at 500 shares — not best-price screenshots.
  • Rules before prices. Every alert lists the settlement-rule differences that could still bite.
  • Free means free. The Telegram channel is free, permanently. If a paid tier ever exists, it will add features — never paywall what is free today.

FAQ

What does it cost?

Nothing. Alerts are free on Telegram.

Does PairWatch place trades?

No. It is an intelligence tool only. You verify both books and trade — or don't — yourself.

Which markets are covered?

Verified pairs today concentrate in Fed decisions and NFL moneylines, where both venues genuinely list the same question. Coverage grows pair by pair, only after rule verification — never by bulk matching.

Why so few alerts?

By design. Most apparent spreads fail the fee, depth, or rule-difference test. A tool that alerts on everything alerts on nothing.
